Stream Agent

A desktop app that turns livestream highlights into short vertical videos.

What it does

Stream Agent runs on the streamer's own computer. It finds highlights in their stream recordings and prepares each one for short-form platforms:

  • Reframes 16:9 gameplay footage to 9:16 vertical
  • Transcribes the audio and burns in captions
  • Drafts a title, caption and hashtags
  • Holds every clip for the streamer to review, then posts the approved ones to the accounts they connected

Who it is for

Streamers who want to share their own highlights on short-form platforms. Each streamer installs Stream Agent on their own computer and connects their own accounts. It is not a hosted service, and it only posts content the streamer has approved.

How TikTok is used

The streamer connects their TikTok account with TikTok's Login Kit. For each clip, the app shows which TikTok account it will post to, and the streamer edits the caption, chooses who can view the video, chooses whether to allow comments, Duet and Stitch, and can disclose commercial content. Nothing is posted until they approve it. If they prefer, they can send the clip to their TikTok drafts to finish in the TikTok app instead.

The app requests user.info.basic (to show the account's name and avatar), video.publish (to post approved clips) and video.upload (to send clips to drafts). It cannot read, browse or delete the streamer's videos.
